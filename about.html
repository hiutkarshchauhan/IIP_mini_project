A legacy of learning & innovation

For more than three decades Meridian has shaped engineers, entrepreneurs and researchers who lead with curiosity and integrity.

Our Story

Built on the belief that ideas change the world

Founded in 1992 with just 120 students and two departments, Meridian has grown into a multidisciplinary institute with a thriving research culture and an alumni network spanning 40 countries.

We believe education should be practical, inclusive and relentlessly curious. Our small-batch classrooms, open labs and mentorship culture keep that belief alive every day.

  • NAAC-style A+ accreditation (demo)
  • Over 200 research papers published every year
  • Dedicated incubator with 60+ student startups
